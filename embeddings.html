So what · why economists care

If a town’s 64 numbers look like a poor town’s numbers, it is probably poor too. No survey needed.

The numbers come from Google DeepMind’s AlphaEarth model. The talk Predicting and monitoring local development from outer space tests how well they predict development goals in Bolivia’s 339 municipalities. The land squares above are drawings, and their 64-number patterns are examples. The Bolivia map is real.
